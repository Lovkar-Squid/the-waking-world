## 0.3.0-beta.3 — the mountain that knew better

One fix, reported from a real colony: **a volcano that could not grow erupted anyway.** With a colony under it the mountain laid no rock — that part was right — but it went on smoking, banging and shaking the screen for the whole of its rise. A colony got the entire eruption and none of the mountain.

Now a volcano will not open on a colony's land at all: the command says so plainly, the Asking Stone already refused, and the world never chooses a site whose foot would fall there. If one is somehow raising itself over a colony anyway — you founded the colony while it was rising — a course that lays not one block is completely silent, and three of them call the eruption off and close its record.

Also: where the mod *decides* something once — where a volcano opens, where a house or a work goes — it now loads the chunk it is asking about rather than treating "not in memory" as "nobody lives there". Those decisions are made a hundred blocks out, past what is loaded.

Everything else in 0.3.0-beta.2 is unchanged. Full changelog: https://github.com/Lovkar-Squid/the-waking-world/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md
